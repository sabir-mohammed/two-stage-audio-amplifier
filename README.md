# Two-Stage Audio Amplifier

## Overview

Designed, simulated and constructed a two-stage audio amplifier PCB capable of amplifying audio from a smartphone and driving an 8 Ω speaker.

The project involved analogue circuit design, PCB layout, simulation and experimental validation using Proteus and laboratory equipment.

## Technical Specifications

- Supply voltage: 12 V DC
- Input source: Smartphone audio output
- Amplifier stages: TL071 active filter and OPA551 output buffer
- Measured voltage gain: Approximately 19 dB
- Measured frequency response: Approximately 10 Hz to 25 kHz
- Output load: 8 Ω speaker

## Design Process

1. Characterised the smartphone's output voltage using a 440 Hz test signal.
2. Designed a two-stage amplifier with active band-pass filtering.
3. Simulated the circuit and designed the PCB in Proteus.
4. Assembled and tested the physical circuit.
5. Compared measured performance with simulated results.

## Results

The completed amplifier achieved approximately 19 dB measured voltage gain, compared with 19.4 dB in simulation.
